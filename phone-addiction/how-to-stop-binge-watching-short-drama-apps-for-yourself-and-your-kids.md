
# [How to Stop Binge Watching Short Drama Apps, for Yourself and for Your Kids](https://ripplefocus.github.io/en/short-dramas/)

Short drama apps like ReelShort and DramaBox end every one minute episode on a cliffhanger and charge coins for the next one, so ten minutes at night easily turns into two hours. This guide covers two cases on Android and iPhone: an adult limiting these apps on their own phone with a bedtime schedule, a daily allowance and an unlock code held by a partner or friend, and a parent limiting them on a child's phone with a schedule, uninstall protection and password protected purchases.
